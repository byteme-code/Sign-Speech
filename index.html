Draw a letter

Draw a capital letter and it is typed and spoken for you. Draw on the board with your finger, a mouse or a pen, or turn the camera on and draw in the air with your index finger.

Draw a letter here with your finger or mouse. Or press Start camera to draw in the air.
Draw a letter on the board.

Draw big, in block capitals. To write several letters at once, draw them side by side from left to right with a small gap between them (and choose a longer pause in the air). You can use more than one stroke, in any order. When you stop drawing for a moment, the letter is typed. In the air: point with your index finger and hold it still until the ring turns green, then draw. Curl or lower your finger to lift the pen, and hold an open hand still for one second for a space. If a letter comes out wrong, tap the right one and it learns your way.
